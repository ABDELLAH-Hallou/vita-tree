# Vita Tree

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

Job applicants prepare a tailored CV and cover letter from their local LaTeX CV collection. Vita CV supplies the AI workflow; Vita Tree makes its steps, drafts, decisions, and results accessible without terminal commands.

## Constraints

Keep per-application isolation, captured configuration, checkpoint validation, source-file identities, and atomic publication. Never replace concurrent edits. Review is the default. An explicit automatic mode accepts AI proposals for the submitted run; it must identify policy decisions separately from human review. Publishing to Git remains a separate user action.

## Product principles

Make the next action obvious. Keep approval controls visible. Explain errors with a recovery action outside graph nodes. Preserve earlier execution history when users select another subset of steps. Support keyboard use, narrow screens, and both light and dark appearance.
